Werch Empire

The werch empire is a fiction Empire from a alternative History called Timeline Furrials, It begins in 764 When king cambyses ruler of the Persian empire conquerers north Nubia and crowns himself king of Nubia, By 673 the Persians are expelled by Nubian groups and a civil war begins and by 657 bce the werch empire is establish under king werch the first and would lay the foundations for the upcoming religion called ramanism by 534 bce his heirs divide the empire in east and west ending the werch legacy.
